Sri Krishna Series: Looking Beyond the Story

The teachings around Sri Krishna invited us to explore the deeper wisdom in the Bhagavatam and the Bhagavad Gita. Rather than treating these narratives only as Purana events, we looked for the spiritual principles they represent.

Krishna's birth and the appearance of Mahamaya opened reflection on the Divine Mother's presence and the creative power that pervades existence. Traditions such as Bathukamma were also explored as ways to recognise and celebrate the life we have been given.

The story of the Pandavas carrying a pumpkin on pilgrimage at Krishna's request made the point simply: sacred travel alone does not transform us. The real pilgrimage is inward — through meditation, awareness, and practice. These teachings ask us to examine our own lives and live with greater awareness.

The teachings around Sri Krishna invited us to explore the deeper wisdom contained in the stories of the Bhagavatam and the Bhagavad Gita. Rather than viewing these narratives merely as events from the Puranas, we were encouraged to look for the spiritual principles and insights they represent.

The account of Sri Krishna's birth and the appearance of Mahamaya opened a reflection on the Divine Mother's presence and the creative power that pervades existence. The discussion also explored the deeper significance of traditions such as Bathukamma, inviting us to recognise and celebrate the life and creative energy that we have been given.

Another memorable teaching used the story of the Pandavas taking a pumpkin on their pilgrimage at Sri Krishna's request. Despite being carried to sacred places and immersed in holy waters, the pumpkin remained unchanged. The message was simple yet profound: travelling to sacred places does not automatically transform us. The real pilgrimage must happen within, through meditation, awareness and inner practice.

These teachings reminded us that the wisdom of Sri Krishna is not limited to stories or scriptures. It invites us to examine our own lives and bring greater awareness to the way we live.

Speak Peace · 9 Days · From Inner Awareness to Compassionate Living

Nonviolent Communication begins with noticing what is alive within us. Across these nine sessions, the journey moves from expressing feelings and needs to self-empathy, deep listening, transforming enemy images, and creating social change. It then carries that understanding into conflict resolution and gratitude. Peace is not only something we ask of others; it is a way of listening, relating, and living.

Day 1: Begin by noticing and expressing what is alive within us. Rather than reacting through blame or judgment, learn to recognise feelings and the needs beneath them, and communicate them honestly.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kQNC8Gyahck

Day 2: Explore how compassionate communication can make life more wonderful. Listening beyond words and recognising the needs behind what people say can bring greater understanding into everyday relationships.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NSNJf76rWeU

Day 3: Lasting change begins within. Observe our own reactions, feelings, judgments, and needs before trying to change another person. Self-awareness becomes the ground for a different way of relating.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6R333eut6g

Day 4: Empathy helps connect hearts. Listen to another person without rushing to advise, correct, or judge. Make room for their feelings and needs, while remaining aware of what is happening within ourselves.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pDVGL5TtNDY

Day 5: Positive change begins with the way we communicate and respond. Recognise the needs behind behaviour and make clear, life-serving requests that invite cooperation rather than force or blame.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EbjzA7sgUI

Day 6: Transform enemy images into compassionate understanding. Look beyond labels and judgments to recognise the human feelings and needs beneath conflict. The work begins by examining our own inner responses.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hyWZEw5gvAk

Day 7: Compassionate communication can extend beyond personal relationships into society. Listen across differences, recognise shared human needs, and take steps towards collective change without losing empathy.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jAkdT9O5QAA

Day 8: Conflict is not simply a contest between winners and losers. Listen for the unmet needs on each side, distinguish observations from judgments, and understand both people and the systems they work within. The examples include mediation, social responsibility, and Marshall Rosenberg's inner preparation before a difficult conversation.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zprQU7sUY1g

Day 9: Make gratitude part of everyday life. Express appreciation by naming the specific action, the need it met, and the feeling it brought. Practise self-empathy instead of self-blame, recognise small acts of cooperation, and let nonviolent communication become a way of life.

Original Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9KxVHNI5thw

Conversation from Heart to Heart

Harika said that discussion about Marshall Rosenberg’s Nonviolent Communication is continuing in the Speak Peace series. She explained that the question of how to state the matter in our heart so that it reaches the other person’s heart, and how truly to become connected, is central to this study. She accepted that even though this method feels easy to listen to, putting it into practice sometimes becomes difficult. However, she said that when it is experienced in life its value becomes clearer.

Connection Beyond Words

The speaker suggested that when true connection forms between two persons, words are not needed every time. In her vision NVC is not merely a collection of some conversation principles; it is deep understanding that brings one person closer to another person. She expressed as her opinion that without bringing forward the word spirituality, spiritual knowledge is visible in Rosenberg’s teachings. This is a spiritual commentary made by the speaker; it is not a quotation given directly from the book.

The Change That Came in Her Understanding About Education

Introducing herself, Harika said that she has had connection with QLU for a long time. She recalled that when she first heard the words of Dr. Newton and Dr. Lakshmi, the feeling arose in her that there was still much more to learn than the education she had known until then. She explained the change she came to from the feeling that education ended after the degree was completed, to the feeling that true study is beginning now.

Inquiry That Grew Through Workshops

She said that in the Past Life Regression workshop she heard about topics such as dreams, stages of soul growth, and Astral Travel. She explained that all these felt like matters she had not been introduced to until then, and that the interest to know more increased. Here she is sharing the wonder that arose in her and her personal inquiry; this article should not be regarded as having given independent scientific confirmation to those topics.

Family Encouragement, the Decision to Learn

Harika mentioned that she came to know about Dr. Newton, Dr. Lakshmi, and Quantum Life University only through her parents. She said that with the family encouraging, she and her sister attended the workshops together. She explained that she decided that after the degree, instead of immediately going for PG study, she would participate in these programs for some time and learn. From her words it is known that that decision reflects the interest she has in continuous study.

From Connection with QLU to Life Experience

She recalled that not only when workshops were held, but with the desire to learn in the remaining time as well, she joined the Life Foundation office. She also mentioned the period when the QLU campus began as part of her experience. She said that she is sharing with others how the matters learned for many years contributed to changes in her life. However, she expressed the humility that not that her journey is completed, but that she too is still learning together with everyone.

Readiness to Receive Knowledge Through Meditation

At the end of the discourse introduction Harika explained her spiritual understanding that through meditation the mind opens and connection with inner knowledge forms. She considered that meditation helps to know which of the matters we hear connects with our inner self. Thereafter she invited for the meditation practice of observing the breath. The subsequent long guided meditation, music, and sankalpa practice were not expanded and written in this teaching article.

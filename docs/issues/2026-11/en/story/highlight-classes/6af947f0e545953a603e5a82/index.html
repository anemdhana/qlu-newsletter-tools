Jivatma–Paramatma Tattva — Granthis and Aham Brahmasmi

Day 575 · Dr. Newton Kondaveti

Continuing the Jivatma–Paramatma theme, Dr. Newton explains the three granthis (Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra) that bind us in sadhana and how they are released. The mahavakya Aham Brahmasmi is illuminated with the gold-ornaments analogy and the river–ocean understanding. The session also includes the symbolism of Goloka and the Gomathas, and practical awareness guidance for Purnima day so that positive vibrations are strengthened.

  • Aham Brahmasmi and gold-ornaments analogy
  • Three granthis: Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra
  • Goloka and Gomathas
  • Purnima — stay balanced and aware

Topic: Jivatma–Paramatma Tattva — Granthis and Aham Brahmasmi

Yesterday’s Message — Crossing Limitations

Yesterday we learned about crossing limitations. We learned about changing the limitation of “I” within us. The more we change the limitations, the further we go. Rising at five in the morning and doing sadhana is also that effort. Even if nothing changes outside, a great change comes within.

Aham Brahmasmi

Aham Brahmasmi — I am Brahman. We must come out of the state of thinking the Atman is separate and Brahman is separate. If that Parabrahma, that Paramatma tattva is gold, then all these ornaments are made of that same gold. Once Narada was speaking with Mahavishnu in Vaikuntha. Vishnumaya means the illusion that this is real. We think we are a river. I am not the river; I am the great ocean.

The Three Granthis — Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra

In sadhana there are three granthis (knots). The first bond is Brahma granthi. This knot must be loosened. Then Vishnu granthi. It is located at the Anahata and Manipura chakras. Vishnu granthi also opens. When going from Vishuddhi to Ajna chakra there is Rudra granthi. This is the biggest knot. Some experience the throat being held and fears arising. With patience and without stopping sadhana, Rudra granthi is released. When these bonds are broken, I am not jivatma, I am Paramatma — Aham Brahmasmi.

Goloka — Gomathas

Here there is a cowshed named Goloka. Goloka is the world where Sri Krishna Paramatma and Radha Mata reside. Going to that Goloka is like truly going to Sri Krishna Paramatma and Radha Mata. In the animal kingdom we regard the Gomathas as mothers. It was the Gomathas who gave milk in childhood. Today everyone should have darshan of the Gomathas and spend some time. We experience wonderful vibrations.

Purnima — Being in Awareness

Today is Purnima. On Purnima day the vibrations are very good. On Amavasya and Pournami days if any negative feeling or anger is taken, it increases tenfold, hundredfold. Therefore one should always remain positive and balanced. One should be in greater awareness. Even while planting trees or walking, every step should be taken with awareness. Let us practice this the whole day today.
